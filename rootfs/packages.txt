# Debian 13 (trixie) packages on top of mmdebstrap's "apt" variant (essential + apt).
# login is no longer Essential in trixie; without it agetty can't log anyone in.

# Not Essential in trixie: needed for fsck of the ext4 partitions and to format config.

# Time zones (timedatectl set-timezone) and locales beyond C.UTF-8 (dpkg-reconfigure

# to wait in DFU mode on the next boot.

iproute2
